I'm Roshita — a B.Sc. Computer Science graduate from Mumbai University, now studying for my M.Sc. in Data Science at Thakur College. Most days I split my time between learning how data science actually works, and helping younger students get their first line of Python to run.

I currently teach Python and robotics at Robomonnk, breaking programming and robotics concepts down into lessons students can actually follow — a habit that's made me better at explaining my own work, not just doing it.

Outside of class, I build. Most of what's below started as a "can I actually make this work" question — a face-recognition attendance system, a Flask app wired up to Google's Gemini API, a property-rental site, a Spotify front-end clone. I like projects small enough to finish and real enough to break.

— Roshita
